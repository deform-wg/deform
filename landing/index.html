The approachCONFIGURATION, NOT SCREENS

A form is a view
of your data.

Product types, account roles, existing records, feature flags: your application already carries the information that determines what a form should ask. Use that information to assemble a configuration, then hand it to <de-form>.

The component renders typed fields, handles validation and change tracking, and can reveal fields when answers change. Your application stays in charge of the data and submission.
